Assumptions and disclaimer

Yield snapshot figures still use only purchase price, area, monthly rent and monthly society maintenance. Gross yield uses potential rent (not vacancy-adjusted).

Mortgage figures use reducing-balance EMI. Cash flow is after operating expenses (society maintenance + property tax + repairs) and after annual loan payments. Rent and those expenses are modelled as flat annual amounts; there is no rent growth, inflation, tax change, prepayment or extra principal.

Scenario paths grow property value, rent and operating expenses from year 0 at the rates you type. Year 0 is the purchase (down payment + acquisition costs). Years 1 to the holding period are end-of-year operating cash flows after actual EMIs. The sale is applied only in the final year: exit value minus selling costs minus remaining loan. After the loan tenure, EMI is zero. Conservative / base / optimistic defaults are editable illustrations, not market forecasts.

Gross property value change is not the same as rental yield, cash flow after financing, net profit or annualised equity IRR. IRR uses the equity cash-flow series only; it is shown as “Not available” when a finite rate does not exist. Property Investment Analyst does not fetch live Bengaluru prices. Nothing on this page is a buy, negotiate or avoid recommendation, nor is it financial, legal or tax advice.
